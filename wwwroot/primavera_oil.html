Oil, Gas & Energy

Deliver capital-intensive energy projects with enterprise-grade project portfolio management built for the demands of the oil and gas industry

Get Started Learn About Primavera P6

Power Your Energy Projects

The oil and gas industry operates some of the world's most complex and capital-intensive projects. From offshore platforms to refineries, pipelines to LNG terminals, these megaprojects require sophisticated project management capabilities to control costs, manage risks, and deliver on schedule. Oracle Primavera is the industry standard for energy project portfolio management.

Whether you're managing upstream exploration, midstream infrastructure, or downstream processing facilities, Primavera provides the visibility, control, and analytics needed to execute successfully in challenging environments.

Benefits

Why Energy Leaders Choose Primavera

Capital Project Control

Manage multi-billion dollar capital projects with integrated cost and schedule control from FID through startup.

Turnaround Management

Plan and execute complex plant turnarounds and shutdowns with precision scheduling to minimize downtime.

Contractor Coordination

Integrate schedules from multiple EPC contractors with consistent reporting and progress measurement.

Risk Management

Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks with Monte Carlo simulation and contingency analysis.

Progress Measurement

Track physical progress with weighted milestones, earned value, and performance metrics.

Portfolio Analytics

Gain enterprise visibility across all capital projects with executive dashboards and portfolio reporting.

Applications

Energy Project Types

Upstream Development

Offshore platforms, subsea systems, drilling campaigns, and field development projects.

Midstream Infrastructure

Pipelines, compressor stations, storage facilities, and LNG terminals across global networks.

Downstream Processing

Refineries, petrochemical plants, and processing facilities requiring complex integration.

Renewable Energy

Wind farms, solar installations, hydrogen facilities, and energy transition projects.
