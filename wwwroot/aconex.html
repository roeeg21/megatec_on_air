Oracle Aconex

Connect teams and processes across the entire construction project lifecycle

Complete Project Control Platform

Oracle Aconex connects teams and processes across the entire construction project lifecycle, capturing a complete project record on one unified platform. With a unique data ownership model where each project organization controls their own workspace, Aconex provides the collaboration and document management capabilities needed for successful project delivery.

From document management to BIM coordination, Aconex provides the tools construction teams need to collaborate effectively, reduce errors, and deliver projects successfully.

Key Capabilities

Document Management

Single document register with strict version control and metadata-driven search for rapid document retrieval.

Unalterable Audit Trail

Complete audit trail capturing all decisions and communications for compliance and dispute resolution.

BIM Model Coordination

Integrated BIM model coordination with clash detection to identify and resolve design conflicts early.

Workflow Automation

Highly configurable workflows for document reviews, approvals, and integrated comment management.

Mobile Jobsite Access

Mobile app for accessing documents, drawings, and models directly from the jobsite.

Bid & Tender Management

Secure bid and tender management with controlled distribution and evaluation tools.

Comprehensive Project Controls

Cost Management

Track and control project costs with integrated financial tools.

Contract Management

Manage contracts and change orders throughout the project.

Quality Management

Inspection and test plan (ITP) management for quality assurance.

Digital Correspondence

Replace traditional email with structured digital correspondence.

Work Packaging

Organize related deliverables into logical work packages.

Project Archives

Complete project archives for long-term retention and compliance.
