Industry Solution

Engineering & Construction

Deliver complex construction projects on time and within budget with enterprise-grade project portfolio management

Get Started Learn About Primavera P6

Build Smarter, Deliver Faster

The construction industry faces unique challenges: complex supply chains, strict regulatory compliance, tight deadlines, and the need to coordinate thousands of tasks across multiple stakeholders. Oracle Primavera provides the robust project management capabilities that engineering and construction firms need to plan, execute, and control projects of any size.

From infrastructure megaprojects to commercial developments, Primavera helps construction companies optimize resources, manage risks, and maintain profitability while delivering quality results.

Benefits

Why Construction Leaders Choose Primavera

CPM Scheduling

Industry-standard Critical Path Method scheduling with support for massive projects containing up to 100,000 activities.

Resource Optimization

Manage labor, equipment, and materials across multiple projects with advanced resource leveling and allocation tools.

Cost Control

Track budgets, monitor spending, and forecast costs with integrated earned value management and variance analysis.

Risk Management

Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks with integrated risk analysis and contingency planning capabilities.

Collaboration

Connect project teams, contractors, and stakeholders with real-time visibility into project status and performance.

Compliance & Reporting

Generate detailed reports for stakeholders, auditors, and regulatory bodies with comprehensive documentation tools.

Applications

Construction Project Types

Infrastructure Projects

Highways, bridges, tunnels, railways, airports, and public utilities requiring multi-year planning and coordination.

Commercial Development

Office buildings, retail centers, hotels, and mixed-use developments with complex stakeholder requirements.

Industrial Facilities

Manufacturing plants, warehouses, and processing facilities requiring precise scheduling and safety compliance.

Residential Construction

Large-scale residential developments, apartment complexes, and housing projects with multiple parallel workstreams.
