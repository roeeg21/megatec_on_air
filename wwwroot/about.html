About Megatec

Your trusted Oracle partner for enterprise project management solutions

Our History

Megatec Advanced Technologies was founded in 1995, providing enterprise solutions for project management excellence. We specialize in Oracle Primavera solutions for project-intensive industries.

Our Mission

We enable companies to deliver projects faster and more cost-effectively through optimization of processes and utilization of the right software solutions.

What Makes Us Different

With a reputation for responsiveness, flexibility, and deep expertise, Megatec has become a leading provider of Oracle Primavera solutions in Israel.

Company Profile

Megatec Advanced Technologies provides products and services that accelerate innovation in the Israeli market. Our aim is to provide complete business solutions to the increasingly complex needs of organizations that need to improve their project delivery efficiency.

As an Oracle Partner, we specialize in implementing and supporting Oracle Primavera P6, Primavera Cloud, and Oracle Aconex solutions. These technology-leading solutions give Megatec's customers a competitive edge in delivering projects on time and within budget.
