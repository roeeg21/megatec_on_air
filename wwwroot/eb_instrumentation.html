Functional Engineering in Process Engineering and Process Control

Engineering Base Instrumentation is the tool for I&E planning. It covers the planning areas process engineering, function and software design, but of course also the electrical engineering.

Engineering Base Instrumentation Features and Advantages

  • P&ID Editing for preliminary planning in process engineering and instrumentation
  • General diagrams and cable run design
  • Pre-calculation
  • Use of I&E typicals
  • Alphanumeric wiring wizards with automatic adjustment of the loops
  • PLC Wizards, bus design
  • Production-adapted revision lists
  • Final documentation
