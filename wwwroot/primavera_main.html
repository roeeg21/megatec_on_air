Oracle Primavera P6 Professional

Enterprise-wide project portfolio management for planning, scheduling, and controlling complex projects

Enterprise Project Portfolio Management

Oracle's Primavera P6 Enterprise Project Portfolio Management is the most powerful, robust, and easy-to-use solution for globally prioritizing, planning, managing, and executing projects, programs, and portfolios. Primavera P6 is an integrated project portfolio management (PPM) solution comprising role-specific functionality to satisfy each team member's needs, responsibilities, and skills.

It provides a single solution for managing projects of any size, adapts to various levels of complexities within a project, and intelligently scales to meet the needs of various roles, functions, or skill levels in your organization and on your project team.

Key Capabilities

CPM Scheduling

Advanced Critical Path Method scheduling with support for up to 100,000 activities per project.

Resource Management

Optimize resource utilization with resource leveling, role-based planning, and capacity analysis.

Earned Value Analysis

Track project performance with comprehensive earned value management and variance analysis.
